Method and limits

The English text encoder uses lightly cleaned original text, independently of WER/CER normalization. CER includes single spaces. Identical semantic inputs score 0; exactly one empty input scores 1 by convention. Empty normalized references have undefined per-clip WER/CER; their insertions still contribute to corpus totals.

Numbers, names, and negation can be wrong despite a small semantic distance. These text comparisons contain no independent audio judgment or human meaning labels. Use the examples to direct review, not to certify correctness.
